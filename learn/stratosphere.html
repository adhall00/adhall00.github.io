The Discovery of the Stratosphere

The discovery of the stratosphere, a story of misadventure and adaptation. Leon Teisserenc de Bort used kites to measure the temperature change with altitude, on one unfortunate day the kite hit a telegraph wire disrupting communication between Rennes and Paris. de Bort had to switch to weather balloons, unmanned balloon with a sandbag attached, the sand bag had a small hole that would allow for a constant rate of ascent, then there was a hook that would tear a hole in the ballloon forming a parachute for the instrument to fall softly to the ground an unassuming citzen could then return the instrument for a small reward. These balloons surpassed the heights kites maxxed out at and in time showed that there is a temperature inversion, a two layer atmosphere, a troposphere and a stratosphere, separated by something that would later be coined the tropopause. (citation: George Ohring, "A most Surprising Discovery")
